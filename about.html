ABOUT THE WINNING CIRCLE

A corner of the internet for stories with heart.

The Winning Circle is a storytelling space for real experiences and imagined worlds. Some stories will make you laugh. Some might make you uncomfortable. Others may remind you of something you have lived through.

Every story has a purpose: to entertain, inspire, and remind us that there is more than one way to win at life.

Meet the storyteller

Winnie is the creator behind The Winning Circle, a space born from a love of stories and the belief that ordinary lives contain extraordinary moments.
